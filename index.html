Meet Our Founder

YourCoffee began with a simple belief: great coffee should be easy to enjoy, anytime, anywhere. What started as a personal passion for brewing and exploring unique blends soon grew into a mission to bring high-quality, thoughtfully crafted coffee to everyone.


From experimenting with beans and roasts to refining every cup for balance and flavor, the journey has been driven by curiosity and a love for the craft. Each step shaped a vision focused on transparency, freshness, and an authentic coffee experience.


Today, yourCoffee continues to grow with that same passion — creating blends that inspire connection, comfort, and the joy of a truly great cup.

Emily Stark

Bean and Beyond

Coffee making begins with roasted and rested coffee beans that are ground to a size suited for the brewing method, then combined with hot water to extract their flavors and aromas; the water dissolves oils, acids, and soluble compounds from the grounds, and the resulting brew is either filtered or left unfiltered depending on the method before being served black or with milk, cream, or sweeteners to create the final cup.

No Cap Cappuccino

Indulge in a timeless classic with our signature, best-selling Classic Cappuccino. Whether you need a morning heart-starter or a cozy afternoon break, this cappuccino is the perfect companion for any occasion.
